Project 03

Satellite Capstone Project

Conceptual system designed to detect electromagnetic disturbances from solar activity and actively counteract them using destructive interference — generating opposing EM waves to protect satellite communications.

A customizable satellite-mounted design was built in Onshape featuring a multi-directional dish system providing 360° coverage for real-time response to varying solar wave frequencies. The concept also explores harvesting solar flare energy as a supplementary power source.

Project 04

Inline Four Internal Combustion Engine

Fully articulated SolidWorks assembly demonstrating mechanical timing and complex motion across a multi-part structural housing.

Features a synchronized 180° crankshaft and overhead camshaft (OHC) linked via a front-end timing drive, modeled cam lobes and rockers that actuate with piston travel, an integrated cooling fan, deep-sump oil pan, and dipstick. Advanced mechanical mates simulate a realistic four-stroke cycle.

Standing Desk Design

Multi-component SolidWorks furniture assembly with integrated mechanical adjustments and peripheral organization.

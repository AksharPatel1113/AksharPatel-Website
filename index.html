Project 01

Drone Challenge

Designed a custom drone to scale in Onshape to visualize custom onboard components. Underwent rapid prototyping across two iterations — 3D printing with Bambu Studio and soldering for full assembly.

Designed wiring diagrams to optimize power output and minimize electrical noise. Mapped flight controller I/O to onboard components accounting for voltage requirements, signal types, and grounding. Produced professional orthographic engineering drawings for all parts.

Earned 150 / 155 on documentation paperwork.

Project 02

Universal Toy Design Project

An in-class project applying Universal Design Principles and Manufacturing Process Principles. The concept: a servo-actuated 3D punching action figure.

Modeled in Onshape within strict size constraints to house all Arduino components. FDM printed using Bambu Studio with iterative fit-checks to tolerance. Programmed servos and buttons in Java with full wiring documentation.

Project 03

Satellite Capstone Project

Conceptual system designed to detect electromagnetic disturbances from solar activity and actively counteract them using destructive interference — generating opposing EM waves to protect satellite communications.

A customizable satellite-mounted design was built in Onshape featuring a multi-directional dish system providing 360° coverage for real-time response to varying solar wave frequencies. The concept also explores harvesting solar flare energy as a supplementary power source.

Project 04

Inline Four Internal Combustion Engine

Fully articulated SolidWorks assembly demonstrating mechanical timing and complex motion across a multi-part structural housing.

Features a synchronized 180° crankshaft and overhead camshaft (OHC) linked via a front-end timing drive, modeled cam lobes and rockers that actuate with piston travel, an integrated cooling fan, deep-sump oil pan, and dipstick. Advanced mechanical mates simulate a realistic four-stroke cycle.

Project 05

Standing Desk Design

Multi-component SolidWorks furniture assembly with integrated mechanical adjustments and peripheral organization.

A worm gear and spur gear system (16DP, 24T) drives four internal shafts for precise vertical height adjustment. Includes a custom drawer assembly, headset holder, speaker mounts, a specialized wheel/casing for mobility, integrated wire management, and a multi-stage leg design for stability throughout the full range of motion.

Additional CAD Projects

A full collection of engineering designs, models, and CAD work across multiple tools and domains.

Drone Challenge

Designed a custom drone to scale in Onshape to visualize custom onboard components. Used rapid prototyping and iterative design — 3D printing with Bambu Studio and soldering for assembly. Created wiring diagrams to optimize power output and minimize electrical noise, mapped flight controller I/O, and produced professional orthographic engineering drawings. Earned 150/155 on documentation.

Onshape 3D Printing Wiring Diagrams Orthographic Drawings

Universal Toy Design Project

An in-class project highlighting Universal Design Principles and Manufacturing Process Principles. Designed a servo-actuated 3D punching action figure in Onshape within strict size constraints, housing all Arduino components. 3D printed using FDM (Bambu Studio) with iterative fit-checks to tolerance. Programmed Arduino servos and buttons in Java and documented full wiring diagrams.

Onshape Arduino Bambu Studio FDM

Satellite Capstone Project

Conceptual system that detects electromagnetic disturbances from solar activity and actively counteracts them by generating opposing EM waves (destructive interference) to protect satellite communications. Designed a satellite-mounted multi-directional dish system in Onshape providing 360° coverage for real-time response. Also explores harnessing solar flare energy as a supplementary power source.

Onshape Systems Design Research Conceptual Engineering

Inline Four Internal Combustion Engine

Fully articulated SolidWorks assembly demonstrating mechanical timing and complex motion. Features a synchronized 180° crankshaft and overhead camshaft (OHC) linked via a front-end timing drive, modeled cam lobes and rockers that actuate with piston travel, a cooling fan, deep-sump oil pan, and dipstick. Advanced mechanical mates simulate a realistic four-stroke cycle.

SolidWorks Assembly Mechanical Mates Motion Study

Standing Desk Design

Multi-component SolidWorks furniture assembly with integrated mechanical adjustments. A worm gear and spur gear system (16DP, 24T) drives four internal shafts for precise vertical height adjustment. Includes a custom drawer assembly, headset holder, speaker mounts, a specialized wheel and casing for mobility, integrated wire management, and a multi-stage leg design for stability across the full range of motion.

SolidWorks Gear Train Furniture Design Assembly
